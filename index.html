Who We Are

About Deborah Circle Ministry

Deborah Circle Ministry (DCM) is a faith-based nonprofit organization headquartered in Kansas, USA. It began as a mentorship initiative focused on helping women maximize their potential, live with purpose, and become agents of positive influence in their communities.

Today, the ministry has expanded its reach to support low-income women in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia through targeted economic empowerment programs.

Together, we are planting seeds of hope, opportunity, and change — one woman, one family, and one community at a time.

Economic Empowerment

We empower low-income women in Ethiopia through financial literacy, personal growth coaching, and entrepreneurial training — helping women build sustainable income and break the cycle of poverty.

Maternal Support

We support public hospitals' maternity wards in Addis Ababa by providing essential supplies to new mothers and their babies, helping ensure a healthy start to life.

Purpose & Direction

Our Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To provide financial support, training, and mentorship that enable women to build sustainable, purpose-driven lives — achieving independence and becoming agents of lasting change in their communities.

Our Mission

To awaken and empower women to live for their God-given purpose through mentorship and intentional development of their gifts and talents. To equip low-income women in Ethiopia with practical tools, training, and financial resources so they can start businesses, gain independence, and uplift their communities.

Our Story

Who We Are

We are a nonprofit organization dedicated to uplifting impoverished women in Ethiopia and mentoring underserved communities in the United States. Our mission is grounded in compassion, education, spiritual growth, and economic empowerment.

What Makes Us Different

Faith-led programs that build both personal and spiritual growth

Education-focused initiatives tailored for long-term impact

Community partnerships that create lasting change

Supportive mentoring relationships across generations

🇪🇹 Where We Serve in Ethiopia

We focus on rural women — supporting education, literacy, and entrepreneurship. Our economic empowerment programs help women start businesses and build sustainable livelihoods.

🇺🇸 Where We Serve in the United States

We support immigrant and underserved communities with mentorship, leadership programs, and life skills — helping women find their footing and flourish.

"Together, we are building a legacy of strength, dignity, and opportunity for women and families around the world."
